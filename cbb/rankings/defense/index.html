How BetWise CBB Team Strength works

Opening rankings

The frozen October 29, 2026 starting order averages KenPom and Bart Torvik preseason ranks equally. A lower average ranks higher; ties use KenPom rank. These sources establish the starting point only and are not refreshed by our daily formula.

Our performance formula

Offensive and defensive efficiency use points per 100 estimated possessions. Possessions average both teams’ FGA − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.475 × free-throw attempts. Offense adjusts for the opponent’s defensive strength; defense adjusts for its offensive strength. Opponent ratings are frozen before each Eastern game day and use only earlier results. Home advantage is set initially at four net points per 100 possessions; neutral sites receive no adjustment. There is no additional SOS bonus, poll bonus, market input or separate D1 loss penalty.

Seven-game form and twelve-game transition

The latest seven D1 performances receive weights of 1.60, 1.50, 1.40, 1.30, 1.20, 1.10 and 1.05, newest first. All earlier games receive 1.00. Preseason influence equals max(0, 1 − completed D1 games ÷ 12). After game 12, only our performance formula remains. Teams with fewer than seven games use the games available.

Rating scale and preseason units

The underlying rating is adjusted offensive efficiency minus adjusted defensive efficiency, less non-D1 penalties. The displayed Strength index is standardized across D1 teams to an average of 50.0 and a standard deviation of 10; it is not a win probability or predicted spread. To turn the average preseason rank into a numerical starting value, we use its normal percentile score multiplied by 10. Internally, that starting value is split equally between offense and defense around a neutral 105-point efficiency baseline. This is an initialization assumption, not an observed offensive or defensive statistic. Separate unit ratings and schedule strength are shown only after verified D1 games.

Non-D1 opponents

Exhibitions are excluded. Verified non-D1 games do not enter efficiency, form or the 12-game count. A win by 15+ earns no bonus and no penalty. Wins by 11–14 deduct 0.5 rating points; wins by 6–10 deduct 1.0; wins by 1–5 deduct 2.0. A loss deducts 3.0 plus 0.1 per point of defeat, capped at 6.0 per game. Penalties accumulate and apply to overall strength only, including during the preseason transition.

Daily publication

Updates run daily at 8 a.m. Eastern with the prior Eastern game day’s verified final results, including D1 postseason games. Daily snapshots are archived. Missing or inconsistent final boxes block an update and preserve the last published list. These initial model parameters have functional checks; they have not yet been optimized through historical predictive backtesting.
