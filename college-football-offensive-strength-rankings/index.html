What goes into our rankings

Our College Football Strength Rankings evaluate offensive and defensive efficiency, opponent quality, strength of schedule, recent performance, scoring margins, quality wins, and losses. A 50.0 strength rating represents the FBS baseline, with higher scores indicating stronger teams. Rankings are updated throughout the season based on completed games.
